What We Can Produce

Our factory handles all common flange types and can manufacture to any customer-supplied drawing.

All Standard Flange Types

Slip-on, weld neck, blind, socket weld, threaded, lap joint — in carbon steel, stainless steel, or alloy steel.

Custom DWG Manufacturing

Submit your AutoCAD DWG or PDF drawings and we will manufacture to exact specification. No catalogue minimums.

Supported Standards

We manufacture to the following standards, or fully custom per your DWG.

Standard Region / Application Notes
AS 2129 Australia / New Zealand Tables D, E, F, H — water, gas, and general service
ANSI / ASME B16.5 United States / International Class 150 to 2500, all common sizes
DIN / EN 1092 Europe PN6 to PN100
JIS B2220 Japan / Southeast Asia 5K to 40K ratings
